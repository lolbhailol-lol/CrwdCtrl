# CrwdCtrl Treks — Vision & Product Direction

CrwdCtrl is the parent platform. **CrwdCtrl Treks** is a vertical — not a separate startup.

## Product question

> What is today's situation at my trek?

## MVP only

1. Today's Trek Status (crowd, weather, trail, parking, forest advisory, entry, last updated)
2. Trek Information
3. Community Updates (timestamp, status, source)

No login, bookings, payments, profiles, dashboards, or backend APIs in v1.

## Destinations

**Lite catalog (current):** Waterfall Treks + Jungle Treks only.  
Fort / mountain / nature-trail categories are out of scope for now.

See `frontend/src/data/treks.js` for the dataset.
