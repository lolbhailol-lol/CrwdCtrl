Frequently asked questions

What is CrwdCtrl?

CrwdCtrl is a community and event discovery platform to find and register for college fests, tech and sports events, running clubs, gym communities, treks, and local meetups near you.

Is CrwdCtrl free to use?

Yes. Browsing and discovering events on CrwdCtrl is free. Some individual events or fests may charge their own registration or ticket fee, which is shown on each event page.

How do I register for a college fest on CrwdCtrl?

Open the fest you are interested in, review the details and competitions, then tap Register and complete the form. You receive a confirmation and a QR ticket where applicable.
