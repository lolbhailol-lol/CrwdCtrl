# 90-Day Content Calendar

A day-by-day plan. Each row is the **"hero" piece** for that day: **Platform · Content Type · Hook · CTA**.

**Daily defaults (assumed on top of the hero piece, not repeated below):**
- **X + Threads:** 1–3 short build-in-public / community posts.
- **Instagram Stories:** 3–5 (BTS, polls, "near you," reposts, countdowns).
- **Engagement:** Reply to all comments/DMs within the first hour.

**Legend** — Content types: Reel, Short, Carousel, Doc (documentary episode), Story-post (founder story), Edu (educational), Recap, UGC, Thread, Live.

> Phases: **Weeks 1–4 = Foundation**, **Weeks 5–8 = Momentum**, **Weeks 9–13 = Scale.** Swap in real, timely hooks (actual events, real numbers) as you go — these are ready-to-use defaults pulled from the idea banks.

## How to keep the calendar alive after Day 90

- **Swap, don't restart.** Replace default hooks with *real* events, *real* numbers, and *real* user stories as they happen — those always outperform templates.
- **Protect the anchors:** the weekly documentary episode, the monthly scoreboard, and the recurring "of the Week" formats. These build the habit and the trust.
- **Mine the idea banks:** [100 viral ideas](./02-viral-content-ideas.md), [50 founder stories](./03-founder-story-content.md), [community](./04-community-content.md), and [educational](./05-educational-content.md) have far more than 90 days of material.
- **Review weekly:** double down on what drives saves, clicks, and DMs; cut what doesn't.
