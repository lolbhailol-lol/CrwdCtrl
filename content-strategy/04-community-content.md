# Community Content — Discovery + FOMO

Goal: make people feel they're **missing out on a life that's available to them right now**, then hand them the easiest possible next step (open CrwdCtrl). Cover treks, run clubs, marathons, theatre, student clubs, and communities.

Two emotional levers run through everything here:
- **Discovery:** "I didn't know this existed near me."
- **FOMO:** "Everyone was there. I wasn't. Never again."

---

## Treks

**Discovery angles**
- "Treks within 2 hours of [city] you've never heard of."
- "Beginner-friendly treks that feel advanced."
- "Hidden monsoon trails near you."
- "How to find a verified trek group (not a random WhatsApp link)."

**FOMO angles**
- "This sunrise had 40 people on the summit. You could've been one."
- "While you slept in, they were here." (sunrise summit b-roll)
- "Every weekend there's a trek leaving without you."
- Weekend recap reels: "What you missed on the trails this weekend."

**Recurring formats**
- *Trail of the Week* — one featured trek with difficulty, distance, vibe, and an open group to join.
- *Organizer spotlight* — the people who run great treks (builds organizer trust + recruits more).
- *Trek-ready checklist* — value content that drives saves.

## FOMO content principles (use responsibly)

1. **Show real abundance.** The feeling should be "so much is happening," not "you're a loser for missing it." Aspirational, not shaming.
2. **Always pair FOMO with the fix.** Never leave someone feeling behind without a one-tap way to act.
3. **Recap relentlessly.** Every event you can document becomes proof + FOMO fuel for the next one.
4. **Localize.** "Near you / in [city]" outperforms generic. Make city-specific cuts where possible.
5. **Use real numbers.** "40 people," "300 registered," "3 spots left" — specifics convert.
