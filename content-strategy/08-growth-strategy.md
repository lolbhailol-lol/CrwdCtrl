# Growth Strategy — 30 / 60 / 90 Day Plans

Three parallel tracks, one timeline:
- **A. Founder Brand** — you become a trusted, followed voice.
- **B. CrwdCtrl Brand** — the product becomes known and credible.
- **C. User Acquisition** — real users, organizers, and communities sign up.

Each phase has a theme, weekly cadence, and concrete targets. Targets are starting benchmarks — adjust to your actual baseline.

---

## Phase 1 — Days 1–30: FOUNDATION ("Show up & document")

**Theme:** Establish presence, voice, and proof. Don't chase virality yet — build the habit and the asset base.

### A. Founder Brand
- Set up/clean profiles on all 5 platforms with consistent bio, photo, and a clear one-liner: *"Building CrwdCtrl — helping people discover events & communities. Documenting the journey."*
- Publish your **origin story** across LinkedIn, X, and a pinned Reel.
- Start the **monthly scoreboard** habit (post #1 at day 30).
- Post 3–5x/week on LinkedIn + daily on X/Threads. Begin replying into 5 founder/investor posts daily.

### B. CrwdCtrl Brand
- Launch the **"Building CrwdCtrl"** documentary series — Episode 1.
- Establish recurring formats: *Trail of the Week, Club of the Week, Community of the Week, This Week Near You.*
- Set up the single link hub (app + waitlist + organizer signup).
- Publish 3 educational carousels (one each: event ops, sponsorships, community).

### C. User Acquisition
- Drive from your home campus (MIT-WPU): onboard 3–5 campus communities/clubs as design partners.
- Manually onboard the first 10 organizers (founder-led; document it as content).
- Seed FOMO/discovery Reels localized to your city.

**30-day targets (benchmarks)**
- 20+ short-form videos shipped; 12+ LinkedIn posts; daily X presence.
- 10 organizers onboarded; 3–5 communities live.
- First 100–300 sign-ups/waitlist.
- 1 documentary episode + 4 recurring-format posts live.

---

## Phase 2 — Days 31–60: MOMENTUM ("Find what works & double down")

**Theme:** Identify winning formats from Phase 1 and scale them. Start converting attention into pipeline.

### A. Founder Brand
- Double down on your 2–3 best-performing story formats.
- Publish 2 long-form founder threads (rejection story, first-100 story) on X + LinkedIn.
- Begin appearing in others' content: comment-leadership, a podcast/Twitter Space or two, collaborations with peer founders.
- Post monthly scoreboard #2 with growth vs month 1.

### B. CrwdCtrl Brand
- Documentary episodes 2–5; cut each into 2–3 Shorts/Reels.
- Ship 1–2 visible product improvements driven by user feedback — turn each into a build-in-public post.
- Publish first **user/organizer story videos** (social proof engine).
- Launch a community challenge (e.g., a 30-day run streak or a "trek a week" series) to generate UGC.

### C. User Acquisition
- Expand to 2–3 more campuses via student ambassadors.
- Scale organizer onboarding to 30–40 total; collect testimonials.
- Run a referral loop ("bring your community / invite a friend").
- Localize content to each new city/campus.

**60-day targets (benchmarks)**
- 50+ total short-form videos; clear winning formats identified.
- 30–40 organizers; 10–15 active communities.
- 1,000+ sign-ups; first repeat-attendance cohort.
- 3+ user/organizer story pieces; 1 community challenge live.

---

## Phase 3 — Days 61–90: SCALE ("Compound & convert")

**Theme:** Turn the content machine into a growth + credibility flywheel. Make the traction legible to investors.

### A. Founder Brand
- Establish thought leadership: a signature POV on the **youth experience economy / college fest economics in India**.
- Pursue earned media: podcasts, founder features, guest posts.
- Build investor surface area: consistent traction posts, a public-ish "open metrics" cadence, warm intros via content.
- Scoreboard #3: 90-day journey recap (great investor + audience artifact).

### B. CrwdCtrl Brand
- Documentary series hits ~12 episodes — a real binge-able asset.
- Publish a **case-study video**: a community or fest that grew measurably via CrwdCtrl.
- Run a flagship moment: a multi-event weekend recap or a "launch a new city" mini-series.
- Systematize UGC: recaps from every powered event feed the content engine.

### C. User Acquisition
- Expand to 5+ campuses/cities.
- 75–100+ organizers; turn best attendees into new organizers (supply flywheel).
- Optimize the funnel: improve content→signup conversion based on 90 days of data.
- Activate community-led growth: communities recruit their own members onto the platform.

**90-day targets (benchmarks)**
- 90+ short-form videos; 12 documentary episodes; consistent multi-platform presence.
- 75–100+ organizers; 25+ active communities.
- 3,000–5,000+ sign-ups; measurable repeat usage; a few "hero" case studies.
- A clean 90-day traction narrative ready for investor conversations.

---

## The growth flywheel (why this compounds)

```
Document the journey  →  Attention & trust  →  Users + organizers sign up
        ↑                                                   ↓
   More stories & receipts  ←  Events happen + communities grow
```

Every event powered → recap content → FOMO → new users + new organizers → more events. The founder story wraps around the whole loop, building the trust that makes each step easier.

## Operating rhythm (all 90 days)

- **Daily:** Post short-form (IG/Shorts), 1–3 X/Threads posts, reply to comments/DMs within the hour.
- **Weekly:** 1 documentary episode, 1 educational piece, 3–5 LinkedIn posts, 1 founder story, review top performers.
- **Monthly:** Public scoreboard, double-down/kill decisions, onboard a new campus/city.
- **Always:** Capture b-roll, collect testimonials, route everything to the link hub.
