# 100 Viral Short-Form Video Ideas

Built for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and X video. Each idea follows: **Hook → Script → B-roll → CTA**.

**Rules of thumb for all 100:**
- The first 1.5 seconds is the hook. Say or show the most interesting thing first.
- Keep most videos 15–45 seconds. Talking-head stories can go to 60–90s.
- Always end with a single, specific CTA. Never two.
- Captions on-screen always (most watch muted).
