# Content Pillars

Ten pillars. Every single post maps to one of these. Aim for a healthy mix each week so no single audience feels ignored. The suggested weekly weighting keeps the founder story present without letting it crowd out product and community value.

| # | Pillar | Primary audience | Weekly weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Founder Journey | Founders, investors, students | ~20% |
| 2 | Building CrwdCtrl (Build in Public) | Founders, investors, organizers | ~15% |
| 3 | College Fest Ecosystem | Students, organizers | ~12% |
| 4 | Community Building | Community builders, organizers | ~10% |
| 5 | Trek Culture | Trek enthusiasts, students | ~8% |
| 6 | Run Club & Marathon Culture | Run club members, students | ~8% |
| 7 | Organizer Playbook (Educational) | Organizers, community builders | ~10% |
| 8 | User & Organizer Stories | Everyone (social proof) | ~8% |
| 9 | Startup Lessons & Failures | Founders, investors | ~5% |
| 10 | Behind The Scenes / Documentary | Everyone | ~4% |

---

## 1. Founder Journey

**The throughline of the whole account.** A student founder building a youth experience platform from MIT-WPU, in real time, with all the messy parts visible.

- **Goal:** Build trust and a personal narrative investors and users can root for.
- **Audience:** Founders, investors, students considering building something.
- **Formats:** Talking-head reflections, voice-over montages, written LinkedIn/X posts, milestone recaps.
- **Sample hooks:**
  - "I started CrwdCtrl because I kept missing every good event on campus."
  - "Month 6 of building a startup as a college student. Here's the honest scoreboard."
  - "Nobody tells you the lonely part of building something."

## 2. Building CrwdCtrl (Build in Public)

The product itself as content: features, decisions, bugs, dashboards, metrics, roadmap.

- **Goal:** Show velocity and competence; convert organizers; signal traction to investors.
- **Audience:** Founders, investors, organizers.
- **Formats:** Screen recordings, "we shipped this" demos, metric screenshots, before/after UI.
- **Sample hooks:**
  - "We just shipped event discovery 'near me.' Here's how it works."
  - "Our registration flow was losing people at step 3. Here's the fix."
  - "From 0 to our first 100 registrations on CrwdCtrl."

## 3. College Fest Ecosystem

The world of college festivals — culture, chaos, behind-the-scenes, the people who run them.

- **Goal:** Own the "college fest" topic; attract students and fest organizing teams.
- **Audience:** Students, fest organizers.
- **Formats:** On-ground reels, fest recaps, "anatomy of a fest" explainers, organizer interviews.
- **Sample hooks:**
  - "What actually goes into running a college fest (it's insane)."
  - "Every college fest has these 7 characters."
  - "The fest committee runs on chai, panic, and group chats."

## 4. Community Building

The art and mechanics of building real communities — the core of CrwdCtrl's mission.

- **Goal:** Attract community builders and position CrwdCtrl as their home base.
- **Audience:** Community builders, organizers.
- **Formats:** Frameworks, teardowns of great communities, founder POV, carousels.
- **Sample hooks:**
  - "Communities don't die from lack of members. They die from silence."
  - "How a 12-person run club becomes a 500-person movement."
  - "The first 10 members decide everything."

## 5. Trek Culture

The outdoors, treks, the groups and organizers who run them, safety, and the magic of showing up.

- **Goal:** Attract trek enthusiasts and trek organizers; widen beyond campus.
- **Audience:** Trek enthusiasts, students.
- **Formats:** POV trek footage, "how to find a legit trek group," organizer spotlights.
- **Sample hooks:**
  - "How to find a trek group that won't get you lost (or scammed)."
  - "The Sahyadris at 5am hit different."
  - "Solo and want to trek? You just need the right group."

## 6. Run Club & Marathon Culture

Run clubs, marathon prep, the social side of running, streaks, and meetups.

- **Goal:** Attract run clubs and runners; show recurring-community use case.
- **Audience:** Run club members, students.
- **Formats:** Meetup recaps, "find a run club near you," runner stories, training-day vlogs.
- **Sample hooks:**
  - "Running is lonely until you find the right club."
  - "Sunday run club > Sunday hangover."
  - "How to start a run club from scratch."

## 7. Organizer Playbook (Educational)

Tactical, genuinely useful content for people who run events and communities.

- **Goal:** Earn organizer trust by giving value first; pull them toward the product.
- **Audience:** Event organizers, community builders.
- **Formats:** Carousels, checklists, short tutorials, "steal this" templates.
- **Sample hooks:**
  - "How to get sponsors for a college event (with no audience yet)."
  - "5 reasons people register and never show up — and how to fix it."
  - "The exact message that gets a sponsor to say yes."

## 8. User & Organizer Stories

Social proof. Real people who found something or grew something through CrwdCtrl.

- **Goal:** Trust + FOMO + conversion via authentic third-party voices.
- **Audience:** Everyone.
- **Formats:** Interviews, testimonial reels, screenshot DMs (with permission), recap reels.
- **Sample hooks:**
  - "She found her trek group on CrwdCtrl. Now they hike every month."
  - "This organizer got 300 registrations in 48 hours."
  - "What people say after their first CrwdCtrl event."

## 9. Startup Lessons & Failures

The honest, specific lessons — especially the failures. The trust-building backbone.

- **Goal:** Build deep trust and relatability; differentiate from hustle-bro content.
- **Audience:** Founders, investors.
- **Formats:** Story-driven talking head, written long-form, "what I'd do differently."
- **Sample hooks:**
  - "We built a feature nobody used. Here's what it cost us."
  - "I pitched 11 organizers and got 11 no's. Number 12 changed everything."
  - "The mistake that almost ended CrwdCtrl in month 2."

## 10. Behind The Scenes / Documentary

The fly-on-the-wall reality — meetings, late nights, onboarding calls, launch days.

- **Goal:** Humanize the brand; create a "series" people follow.
- **Audience:** Everyone.
- **Formats:** Vlogs, b-roll montages, "day in the life," episode-style content.
- **Sample hooks:**
  - "Day in the life of a student building a startup (real, unedited)."
  - "Come with me to onboard a trek organizer."
  - "Launch day for our new city. It did not go as planned."
