# Platform-Specific Strategies

Each platform has a different job. Don't cross-post blindly — adapt. Below: **what to post, frequency, goals, and format** for each.

**The roles at a glance:**

| Platform | Primary job | Primary audience |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | Discovery, FOMO, community proof, user acquisition | Students, trekkers, runners, communities |
| LinkedIn | Founder credibility, organizer B2B, investor signal | Organizers, founders, investors, sponsors |
| YouTube | Depth, documentary, searchable trust | Everyone (long-term compounding) |
| X (Twitter) | Build-in-public, founder network, real-time | Founders, investors, tech/startup crowd |
| Threads | Casual conversation, community, reach experiments | Students, early adopters, communities |

---

## Instagram

**What to post**
- Reels: FOMO/discovery (Groups A–D, I, J from the viral bank), event recaps, user stories, trend formats.
- Carousels: educational (organizer/community), listicles ("5 treks near you"), frameworks.
- Stories (daily): behind-the-scenes, polls, "this weekend near you," reposts of user/organizer content, countdowns.
- Highlights: Treks, Run Clubs, Fests, For Organizers, The Journey.

**Frequency**
- Reels: 5–7/week (this is the growth lever — prioritize).
- Carousels: 2–3/week.
- Stories: 3–5/day.

**Goals**
- User acquisition + discovery (top of funnel).
- FOMO → app installs/sign-ups.
- Community social proof.

**Format notes**
- Hook in first 1.5s; captions always on.
- Localize ("near you / in [city]").
- Strong, single CTA → link in bio (use a link hub: app + waitlist + organizer signup).
- Reply to every comment fast in the first hour.

## LinkedIn

**What to post**
- Founder journey & lessons (text posts, 800–1,500 chars, strong first line).
- Build-in-public milestones with real numbers.
- Organizer-focused educational carousels (sponsorships, event ops, community).
- Investor-relevant insight: market POV on India's youth experience economy, college fest economics.
- Wins, gratitude posts (tag organizers/partners), monthly scoreboards.

**Frequency**
- 3–5 posts/week (text-first; 1–2 carousels/week).
- Comment meaningfully on others' posts daily (network growth).

**Goals**
- Founder credibility + investor radar.
- B2B: attract organizers, sponsors, partners.
- Recruiting (team + ambassadors).

**Format notes**
- First line is the hook — no "I'm excited to announce."
- Short paragraphs, white space, one idea per post.
- End with a question to drive comments (the algorithm rewards conversation).
- Specifics > slogans. Numbers, names, real moments.

## YouTube

**What to post**
- Flagship "Building CrwdCtrl" documentary series (5–10 min episodes).
- Founder-vlog day-in-the-life.
- Educational tutorials (organizer how-tos — searchable, evergreen).
- User/organizer case studies.
- Shorts: repurposed cuts from Reels + long-form.

**Frequency**
- Long-form: 1/week (consistency > volume).
- Shorts: 3–5/week (repurpose existing short-form).

**Goals**
- Deep trust + the long-term compounding asset (search + binge).
- Investor/organizer due-diligence material ("watch the journey").
- SEO: rank for "how to plan a college fest," "trek groups near [city]," etc.

**Format notes**
- Titles + thumbnails carry 80% of clicks — invest here.
- First 30 seconds must hook + promise payoff.
- Use chapters; pin a CTA comment.
- Build playlists per series/topic.

## X (Twitter)

**What to post**
- Build-in-public: daily/near-daily updates, shipped features, metrics, learnings.
- Founder threads: stories, lessons, breakdowns (the rejection story, the first-100 story).
- Short reactions, hot takes on the events/community space.
- Replies into founder/investor/startup conversations (network leverage).
- Screenshots: dashboards, DMs, milestones.

**Frequency**
- 1–3 posts/day + active replies.
- 1–2 threads/week.

**Goals**
- Founder network + investor discovery (many Indian investors are active here).
- Build-in-public credibility.
- Real-time documentation of the journey.

**Format notes**
- One idea per tweet; threads for stories.
- Hooks matter; lead with the most surprising line.
- Engage > broadcast. Reply to bigger accounts with genuine value.
- Pin your best founder thread.

## Threads

**What to post**
- Casual, conversational versions of your X/LinkedIn thoughts.
- Community questions ("What's the best run club in [city]?").
- Behind-the-scenes musings, polls, "building today" notes.
- Repurposed hooks to test what resonates.

**Frequency**
- 1–3 posts/day (low-effort, high-frequency).

**Goals**
- Reach experiments + community conversation.
- Reach a younger, casual audience overlapping with IG.

**Format notes**
- Be human and informal; Threads rewards personality and replies.
- Ask questions; jump into relevant conversations.
- Cross-pollinate with Instagram audience.

## Cross-platform operating system

- **Create once, adapt everywhere.** One core idea → reel (IG/Shorts) + carousel (IG/LinkedIn) + text post (LinkedIn/X/Threads) + clip (YouTube Shorts).
- **The funnel:** X/LinkedIn build *credibility* → Instagram/YouTube drive *discovery & trust* → all roads point to **the app + waitlist + organizer signup**.
- **Native-first.** Re-edit per platform (aspect ratio, captions, hooks). Never post a visible TikTok watermark or "link in bio" on LinkedIn.
- **One link hub.** Keep a single bio link (app store / web app / waitlist / "for organizers") so every platform routes to conversion.
- **Weekly review.** Double down on top performers; kill formats that don't move saves/clicks/DMs.
